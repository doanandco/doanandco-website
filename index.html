Our Philosophy

The tool should never
own the hand
that holds it.

We didn't build Doan & Co because we hate technology. We built it because we love what life feels like without it running the show.

The average person spends over 7 hours a day on screens — not because they chose to, but because the technology was designed to keep them there. We design the opposite.

Built on
three beliefs.

01

Technology is a
means, not an end.

Every product we make exists to help you do something in the real world — then get out of your way.

02

Attention is your
most valuable asset.

We will never design anything that competes for your attention. We help you protect it.

03

The best moments
happen offline.

Our success is measured not by engagement metrics — but by how much time you spend away from screens.
