# DOAN & CO — Complete Setup Guide
## From Zero to Live Website (Beginner Friendly)
### Estimated time: 45–60 minutes total

---

  doanandco/

  ├── app/index.html      ← One Good Thing app page

## STEP 1: SIGN UP FOR GITHUB (Free — 5 minutes)
  
GitHub is where your website files live in the cloud. It's like Google Drive 
but for websites.

1. Go to: https://github.com
2. Click "Sign up" (top right)
3. Enter your email, create a password, choose a username
   (suggestion: something like "doanandco" or your name)
4. Verify your email address
5. You're in — GitHub is free and you'll stay on the free plan

---

## STEP 2: CREATE A REPOSITORY (5 minutes)

A "repository" (or "repo") is just a folder for your project on GitHub.

1. Once logged into GitHub, click the green "New" button 
   (or go to github.com/new)
2. Name it: doanandco-website
3. Make sure "Public" is selected
4. Check the box: "Add a README file"
5. Click "Create repository"

You now have an empty project folder on GitHub.

---

## STEP 3: UPLOAD YOUR FILES TO GITHUB (10 minutes)

1. You're now looking at your new repository page
2. Click the "Add file" button → choose "Upload files"
3. Open your computer's file browser and find the "doanandco" folder 
   you downloaded
4. Select ALL the files and folders inside "doanandco" 
   (not the folder itself — select what's inside it)

   TIP: Press Ctrl+A (Windows) or Cmd+A (Mac) to select everything

5. Drag them all into the GitHub upload area
6. Wait for them all to upload (you'll see progress bars)
7. Scroll down, type a message like "first upload" in the box
8. Click "Commit changes"

All your files are now on GitHub.

---

## STEP 4: SIGN UP FOR NETLIFY (Free — 5 minutes)

Netlify is the service that takes your files from GitHub and turns them 
into a real website on the internet.

1. Go to: https://netlify.com
2. Click "Sign up"
3. IMPORTANT: Choose "Sign up with GitHub" — this connects them together
4. Authorize Netlify to access your GitHub (click "Authorize")
5. You're now in Netlify's dashboard

---

## STEP 5: DEPLOY YOUR SITE (5 minutes)

This is the magic step — one click and your site goes live.

1. In Netlify, click "Add new site" → "Import an existing project"
2. Click "GitHub"
3. Find and click "doanandco-website" (the repo you just created)
4. Leave all settings as-is (defaults are correct)
5. Click "Deploy site"

Netlify will show a spinning loading animation for about 30–60 seconds.

When it's done, you'll see a green "Published" status and a weird URL like:
  https://random-words-123456.netlify.app

THAT'S YOUR WEBSITE! Click the link — it's live.

---

## STEP 6: ENABLE YOUR ADMIN LOGIN (5 minutes)

Before you can edit your site, you need to turn on the login system.

1. In Netlify, click "Site settings" (in the top menu)
2. In the left sidebar, scroll down to find "Identity"
3. Click "Enable Identity"
4. Scroll down to "Registration" — change it to "Invite only" 
   (this means only you can log in)
5. Scroll further to "Git Gateway" — click "Enable Git Gateway"

Now invite yourself:
6. Go back to "Identity" in the sidebar
7. Click "Invite users"
8. Enter your own email address
9. Click "Send"
10. Check your email — you'll get an invite link
11. Click the link and set your password for the admin panel

You can now log into your CMS at:
  https://your-site-name.netlify.app/admin

---

## STEP 7: CONNECT YOUR DOMAIN (15 minutes)

This changes your URL from the random one to doanandco.com.

PART A — Tell Netlify your domain:
1. In Netlify, click "Domain settings"
2. Click "Add a domain"
3. Type: doanandco.com
4. Click "Verify" then "Add domain"
5. Netlify will give you two "nameserver" addresses that look like:
     dns1.p01.nsone.net
     dns2.p01.nsone.net
   (Copy these — you'll need them in a moment)

PART B — Point your domain to Netlify:
(These steps depend on WHERE you bought your domain. Common options below.)

  IF YOUR DOMAIN IS FROM GODADDY:
  1. Log into GoDaddy
  2. Click your name (top right) → "My Products"
  3. Find doanandco.com → click "DNS"
  4. Scroll to "Nameservers" → click "Change"
  5. Select "Custom" nameservers
  6. Delete the existing ones and enter Netlify's nameservers
  7. Click "Save"

  IF YOUR DOMAIN IS FROM NAMECHEAP:
  1. Log into Namecheap
  2. Click "Domain List" → click "Manage" next to doanandco.com
  3. Find "Nameservers" → choose "Custom DNS"
  4. Enter Netlify's nameservers
  5. Click the checkmark to save

  IF YOUR DOMAIN IS FROM SQUARESPACE DOMAINS:
  1. Log into Squarespace
  2. Go to Domains → click doanandco.com
  3. Click "DNS Settings"
  4. Find Nameservers → click "Use custom nameservers"
  5. Enter Netlify's nameservers

WAITING: After saving nameservers, it can take anywhere from 10 minutes to 
48 hours for the change to take effect across the internet. This is normal.
Your site at the random netlify.app URL still works in the meantime.

## STEP 8: HOW TO EDIT YOUR WEBSITE

Once your admin is set up, editing is easy.

1. Go to: https://doanandco.com/admin
   (or your netlify.app URL while waiting for domain)
2. Log in with your email and password
3. You'll see a dashboard with sections:
   - Site Settings → Edit homepage text, social media links
   - Journal Posts → Write and publish new blog posts
   - App Page → Update app info, add App Store links when ready
   - 7 Day Detox Page → Add content to each day
   - Community Page → Update waitlist info

TO EDIT TEXT:
1. Click the section you want to edit
2. Change the text directly — it works like a Word document
3. Click "Publish" or "Save" when done
4. Your website updates automatically within 1–2 minutes

TO ADD IMAGES:
1. When editing any page, look for "image" fields
2. Click "Choose an image" or the upload button
3. Upload from your computer (JPG or PNG files work best)
4. The image appears on your site after saving

TO WRITE A BLOG POST:
1. Click "Journal Posts" in the admin sidebar
2. Click "New Journal Post"
3. Fill in: Title, Date, Category, Excerpt, and Body text
4. Upload a featured image if you have one
5. Click "Publish"
6. The post appears on your journal page immediately

## STEP 9: REPLACING PLACEHOLDER IMAGES

Your site currently has gray placeholder boxes where photos should go.
Here's how to replace them:

For FREE professional photos, use:
  https://unsplash.com — search terms like:
  - "morning coffee journal" 
  - "forest path nature"
  - "person reading book"
  - "sunrise landscape"
  - "friends outdoors"

1. Download the photo (click the down arrow on Unsplash)
2. Log into your admin at /admin
3. Click the page you want to update
4. Find the image field and upload your downloaded photo
5. Save

ALTERNATIVELY — for pages like the app page and detox page that have 
placeholder boxes in the HTML, you can replace them by:
1. In GitHub, navigate to the file (e.g., app/index.html)
2. Click the pencil icon to edit
3. Find the placeholder div and replace with an <img> tag
4. Commit the change

(If this feels too technical, a Fiverr developer can do this in 30 minutes 
for a very small fee)

## STEP 10: ADDING EMAIL SIGNUPS THAT ACTUALLY WORK

The newsletter forms on your site currently don't send emails anywhere.
To make them work, use Mailchimp (free up to 500 subscribers):

1. Sign up at: https://mailchimp.com (free plan)
2. Create an "Audience" (your email list)
3. Create a form and get the embed code or form action URL
4. In each of your HTML files, update the form action to your Mailchimp URL

For a non-technical solution: 
Use Netlify Forms (already partially set up). Just add this to your 
form tags: netlify data-netlify="true"

Netlify will capture form submissions and email them to you — visible 
in your Netlify dashboard under "Forms".

## TROUBLESHOOTING

"My site shows a 404 error"
→ Files may not have uploaded correctly. Go to GitHub and check that 
  index.html is in the root (main) folder, not inside a subfolder.

"I can't log into admin"
→ Make sure you clicked the invite link in your email before trying to log in.
  If expired, go to Netlify → Identity → Invite users again.

"My domain isn't working after 48 hours"
→ Double-check the nameservers are entered exactly as Netlify gave them.
  Contact your domain registrar's support — they can verify it's set up right.

"Images aren't showing up"
→ The file path matters. Images must be in assets/images/ and referenced 
  correctly in your HTML.

"I want to change something but don't know how"
→ Most visual changes are possible through the admin panel.
  For structural changes (layout, colors, fonts), a developer can help —
  or feel free to ask Claude for guidance.
