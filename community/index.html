What Is This?

A community that
helps you spend
less time online.

Yes — we're building an online community about spending less time online. The irony is not lost on us. The idea is that the online time you do spend here is intentional, purposeful, and helps you live better everywhere else.

🤝

Accountability partners

Pair with someone working on the same offline challenge. Check in once a week. Real human accountability, not app streaks.

📅

Monthly challenges

A new group challenge every month. Phone-free Sundays. Analog August. Morning pages. Do them alone or with the group.

💬

Real conversations

No algorithm. No viral posts. Just a simple forum for people sharing what's working, what's hard, and what they're learning.

📚

A curated resource library

Books, articles, tools, and research on digital wellness — hand-picked and organized so you're not searching through noise.
